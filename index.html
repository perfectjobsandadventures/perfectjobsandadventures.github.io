FAQ

Good questions

What does the Basic Arrival Program include?

For $75 AUD you get guidance on getting your Tax File Number, help setting up your bank account, Working With Children Check guidance, SIM card setup and an orientation to public transport. We also point you to the essentials: MyGov, superannuation information, local orientation and emergency information.

What extra do I get with Premium?

Premium includes everything in Basic, plus an airport transfer when you land and 3 nights of hostel accommodation. It costs $499 to $799 AUD.

Why is Premium a price range?

You choose the extras. Orientation tours, local SIM data bundles and welcome event tickets set where your package sits between $499 and $799 AUD. Tell us what you want and we'll confirm your price before you book.

Who are your packages for?

Anyone aged 18 to 35 arriving in Australia: working holiday makers, backpackers, au pairs, gap year travellers and international students.

Can you help me book trips and transport?

Yes. We help with adventures like the Great Barrier Reef, the Whitsundays, K'gari, Uluru, surf camps, skydiving and scuba diving, plus hostels, hotels, campervans, car hire, airport transfers and bus and rail passes.

Can you help me find a job?

Our job board, plus help with your resume, cover letter and interview prep, is coming soon. Ask to be told when it's live.

Do you give visa advice?

No. For visa rules, always check the official Department of Home Affairs website.

How do I get in touch?

Message us on WhatsApp, call 0413 161 195 or email info@perfectadventures.com.au.
